The posture checks

Ten questions, asked every time you connect

Wireless encryptionhigh
The link is open, or protected by long-broken security.
Forced login pagesmedium
Traffic is held until you sign in on the network's page.
Intercepted trafficcritical
Someone is standing between you and the sites you visit.
Hidden relaysmedium
An unseen middleman passes unencrypted traffic through.
Faked name lookupshigh
The network lies about where websites really live.
Open managementmedium
The router's admin doors are reachable by anyone nearby.
Impersonated gatewayhigh
The router's identity does not match what the network claims.
Traceable devicelow
Your device broadcasts a permanent, trackable identifier.
Twin hotspotshigh
Another access point borrows the same network name.
Working connectioninfo
Confirms the internet is actually reachable.
When a check cannot run, it is skipped rather than guessed, so a blocked network never produces a false alarm.

Verdict & scoring

From ten answers to one decision
